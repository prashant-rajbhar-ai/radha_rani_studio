About Me

📍 Based in Vishukiya, Ratanpura, Mau (221706), Uttar Pradesh, Radha Rani Studio offers professional wedding photography and videography services across Mau and nearby areas. My name is Prashant, and I believe every wedding is a beautiful story worth capturing forever.

I focus on genuine emotions, candid moments, and timeless memories that reflect your unique love story. From laughter to heartfelt traditions, every detail is captured with creativity and care to make your special day unforgettable.

With a background in Computer Applications (BCA), I use modern tools and techniques to enhance the quality of my photography and videography. From high-resolution editing to cinematic color grading, every detail is refined to deliver visually stunning results.

This technical approach ensures that your wedding photos and films are not only beautiful but also professionally crafted, preserving your memories in the best possible quality for years to come.
